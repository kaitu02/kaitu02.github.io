About me

I am currently an Assistant Professor in School of Mathematical Sciences at Shenzhen University. In 2020, I received my Ph.D. degree in Operational Research and Cybernetics from Beijing University of Technology, under the supervision of Prof. Haibin Zhang. I obtained my M.S. in Operational Research and Cybernetics under the supervision of Prof. Fuquan Xia in 2016 and B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematic in 2013, both from Sichuan Normal University. My link on Google Scholar.

Research Interests

  • My research focuses on continuous optimization and its application in variational inequalities (VI), nonconvex optimization, and decision making under uncertainty.
